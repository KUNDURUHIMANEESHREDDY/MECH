Phase 57 is a solid continuation, and the result changes what Phase 58 should optimize for.

The key achievement is that MECH now evaluates three qualitatively different prospective outcomes—positive transfer, negative transfer, and calibrated abstention—rather than testing only successful predictions. The reported suite gives 99.39% positive-transfer fidelity, 98.57% negative-transfer fidelity, 100% abstention fidelity, and 0.59% cross-trial heterogeneity. It also has an automatic downgrade path when an uncalibrated failure occurs.
