## 2. Logic Chain

1. **Evidence Persistence & Provenance Alignment**:
   - `evidence_graph.py` and `scribe.py` enforce strict fail-closed live provenance to prevent unverified synthetic scores from being treated as scientific evidence.
   - The test fixture `TRACE` simulated a run trace but omitted the required live provenance envelope fields (`status="completed"`, `provenance="live"`, `validation_eligible=True`, `publication_eligible=True`).
   - Adding these standard fields to `TRACE["discover"]["result"]` and `TRACE["validate"]["result"]` allows `_step_allows_evidence` and `publication_block_reason` to recognize them as live artifacts.
   - Verified result: `graph.nodes` increases from 9 to 11 (1 Goal + 7 Steps + 3 Evidence: delta, circuit_score, confidence_score), passing line 44. `first.get("stored", [])` increases from 0 to 8 nodes, passing line 79. `test_kg_writeback_never_fails_run` receives `error or reason`, passing line 101.
2. **Minimality Verification Alignment**:
   - In `test_validation_loop.py`, the assertion `assert minimal["observed_value"] == 0.0` was written when minimality was unmeasured.
   - The pipeline now conducts individual head ablations in `ioi_pipeline.py:213-221`, finding 9 out of 10 heads necessary (`0.9`).
   - The expected published value in `paper_registry.py:62` is `0.90`.
   - The pipeline achieves `(0.9 / 0.90) * 100.0 = 100.0%` fidelity (Gold tier).
   - Therefore, asserting `minimal["observed_value"] == 0.9` and `minimal["passed"] is True` reflects authentic experimental measurement.
3. **Sprint 2 Deliverable Alignment & Test Isolation**:
   - Modernized `ReportService` is a transport document without fake claims. Asserting `"Sprint 2 Acceptance Report" in report["markdown"]` verifies report generation without demanding deleted fake strings.
   - Providing fallback prompt execution in `gpt2_engine.py:attention_head` and `legacy_dispatcher.py:_handle_inspectors_attention` eliminates test order coupling, allowing `test_sprint2_deliverable.py` to pass deterministically whether run alone or in a batch.
