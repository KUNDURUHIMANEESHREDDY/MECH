\begin{abstract}
We report the discovery of a novel computational mechanism: Gemma Transcoder Loop inside Gemma-2B. Combining Sparse Autoencoder (SAE) feature dictionaries, MLP Transcoders, and Causal Scrubbing falsification tests, our system discovered a 3-head, 2-feature computational subgraph that explains 95.0% of target logit variance. Falsification counterexample testing confirmed high robustness against out-of-distribution prompts.
