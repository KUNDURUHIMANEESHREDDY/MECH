What Phase 58 establishes

It gives evidence that MECH is not simply memorizing individual model/task pairs.

But there is one important remaining loophole:

The held-out grid is still defined by MECH's own benchmark construction.

So Phase 59 should remove MECH's control over what is tested.

Phase 59 — Independent Adversarial Prospective Replication

The scientific question becomes:

Can an independent evaluator deliberately choose cases that are most likely to break MECH's frozen mechanistic law, and does MECH remain calibrated under that adversarial selection?

This is stronger than ordinary OOD testing.
